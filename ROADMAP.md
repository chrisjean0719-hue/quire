# Roadmap

Quire plans nested folio signatures for a hand-bound book. Five updates remain after this first slice, about 35 hours.

## Quarto and octavo sheets (6 hours)

Lay a signature onto one large sheet, with several pages on each side, instead of one folio sheet per four pages. Support 8-page and 16-page sheet patterns.

## Parent sheet and grain (6 hours)

Fit the printed sheet onto a larger parent sheet, report the trimmed waste, and warn when the fold fights the paper grain.

## Creep (5 hours)

Shift inner folios outward by a chosen thickness so the fore-edge stays even after folding.

## Sheet diagram (6 hours)

Write an SVG drawing of each sheet side with the page numbers in place.

## Plan comparison (4 hours)

Compare two book specs by sheet count, blank padding, and trimmed waste.

## Padding report (3 hours)

Print where each blank slot sits in the bound book, and offer a page count that removes the padding.

## Diagram marks (3 hours)

Add fold, head, and crop marks to the SVG sheet diagram.

## Reading proof (2 hours)

Walk the imposed sheets in reading order and fail the plan if a page is missing, repeated, or out of sequence.

The quarto and octavo sheet is the next update. The current planner only nests folio sheets.
